_Also asked as:_ expected return; roi; profit kitna; how much will I earn
_Handling:_ AI Auto

Operational (mature) properties: you earn rental income plus forecasted capital appreciation over time.
Developmental properties: forecasted capital appreciation during construction, with rental income beginning once the project is operational.
Returns are forecasted and depend on the specific project, market conditions, and how long you stay invested. They are not fixed or guaranteed, and values can move in both directions. I can share the forecasted numbers for a specific project if you would like.

### [INV-02] What is the difference between mature and developmental properties?
_Also asked as:_ mature vs developmental; operational vs under construction; difference in project types
_Handling:_ AI Auto

Mature (operational) properties are already built and generating rent, so you earn steady rental income plus capital appreciation. Developmental properties are still under construction, so you enter at an early stage at a lower price and earn from capital appreciation as the project completes. There is no rental income during the development phase. Developmental projects target higher growth but carry construction and timeline risk.

### [OWN-02] What are non-demarcated area rights?
_Also asked as:_ non demarcated; area rights meaning; what is non demarcated ownership
_Handling:_ AI Auto

Non-demarcated area rights mean you own a number of square feet in a project that are not linked to a specific unit. This lets you start small and add more over time. Your rights include buying more area, converting to a specific unit once you hold enough, selling after project completion, and earning a proportionate share of rental income once the project is operational.
