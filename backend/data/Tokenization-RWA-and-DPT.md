# Tokenization, Real World Assets, and DPT

Knowledge for the assistant. Use this to answer questions about tokenization, real-world assets, the global landscape, and DPT. For anything needing the very latest numbers, use web results too.

## What tokenization means, in plain words

Tokenization is turning ownership of a real asset, like a building, gold, or a bond, into digital shares that are recorded securely and can be owned, tracked, and transferred easily. Instead of one person buying a whole property, many people can each own a verified digital share of it. The goal is to make ownership more accessible, transparent, and easier to move than traditional paperwork allows.

## What Real World Assets (RWA) means

Real World Assets are physical or traditional financial assets brought into digital, shareable form: real estate, gold and commodities, government bonds and treasuries, private credit, infrastructure such as solar, and more. "RWA tokenization" is the global movement of putting these real assets on-chain so they can be owned in pieces, verified, and traded.

## The global picture today

- RWA tokenization has moved from small experiments to real institutional adoption. Industry reporting describes 2024 to 2025 as the point tokenization shifted from pilots to scaled institutional use.
- The on-chain RWA market, excluding stablecoins, grew roughly 380%, almost fivefold, over three years, to around 24 to 30 billion US dollars.
- The largest categories today are tokenized government treasuries, private credit, real estate, and commodities.
- Major institutions are already in. Examples widely cited include BlackRock's tokenized fund (BUIDL) and Apollo's tokenized private credit.

## Why it matters

McKinsey estimates that more than 400 trillion US dollars of the world's assets are illiquid, meaning real estate, private credit, infrastructure, commodities, and private equity that are expensive to transfer, hard to price in real time, and out of reach for most people. Tokenization is the way that value gets opened up so ordinary people can access it. Real estate is one of the largest of these asset classes.

## The stages of RWA, how it is unfolding

1. Pilots and proofs: early experiments by banks and startups.
2. Institutional scale, 2024 to 2025: large funds and banks tokenizing treasuries and private credit. This is where the world is now.
3. Broad adoption: many asset classes, deep liquidity, and everyday access for regular investors. This is where it is heading.

## What unlocks the next stage

Three things need to mature: clear regulation (in the US and EU, and in markets like Pakistan through PVARA, the virtual asset regulator), institutional-grade custody and settlement so assets are held safely, and deep secondary-market liquidity so people can enter and exit easily.

## The future of RWA, the forecasts

Forecasts vary because different reports count different things, but they all point to trillions:
- McKinsey: about 2 to 4 trillion US dollars by 2030.
- BCG: about 16 trillion US dollars by 2030, roughly 10% of global GDP. BCG with Ripple: about 9.4 trillion by 2030, rising to about 18.9 trillion by 2033.
- Standard Chartered: more than 30 trillion US dollars by 2034.
- Deloitte: tokenized real estate alone about 4 trillion US dollars by 2035, up from under 0.3 trillion in 2024.

The exact number is debated, but the direction is not: RWA is scaling fast, and real estate sits at the centre of it.
